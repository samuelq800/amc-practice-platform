AMC/BMO 平台说明 / About the Platform

AMC 10/12 + BMO1 Practice Center

About This Platform

面向 AMC 10/12 与 BMO1 备赛的校内数学训练平台
A School-Based AMC 10/12 and BMO1 Training Platform

本平台由数学社维护，面向准备 AMC 10/12 与 BMO1 的同学提供系统化真题练习、AMC 全卷模拟和学习表现分析。 AMC 模块用于选择题训练与限时模拟；BMO1 模块用于证明题分类训练，并与 AMC 题库清晰分开。
This platform is maintained by the Math Club. It supports students preparing for AMC 10/12 and BMO1 through structured past-paper practice, AMC full mock exams, and performance diagnostics. The AMC module focuses on multiple-choice practice and timed mocks; the BMO1 module is a separate proof-problem practice bank.

维护方 / Maintained By

由数学社维护
Maintained by the Math Club

平台内容整理、功能迭代和使用反馈收集由数学社持续维护，用于支持校内数学学习共同体建设。
The Math Club maintains content organization, feature updates, and feedback collection to support the school mathematics community.

题库范围 / Problem Bank

收录 AMC 10/12 与 BMO1 训练题
AMC 10/12 and BMO1 Practice

AMC 题库按年份、卷别、题号、知识点和难度区间组织；BMO1 题库作为独立训练入口，按年份、知识点和难度整理。
AMC problems are organized by year, form, problem number, topic, and difficulty band; BMO1 has a separate practice entry organized by year, topic, and difficulty.

练习方式 / Practice Modes

支持 AMC 练习与 BMO 训练
AMC Practice and BMO Training

AMC 单题练习适合按知识点查漏补缺；AMC 全卷模拟提供 75 分钟倒计时和模拟计分。BMO1 训练作为独立入口展示证明题，导出报告会同时保留 AMC 与 BMO1 的学习记录。
AMC single practice supports targeted review, and AMC full mock exams include a 75-minute timer and scoring. BMO1 training is a separate proof-problem entry, while export reports retain both AMC and BMO1 learning records.

学习分析 / Learning Analytics

适合 AMC 与 BMO1 阶段训练
Built for AMC and BMO1 Readiness

平台会在本机保存 AMC 与 BMO1 作答记录，并根据正确率生成知识点表现与复习建议，帮助学生从“刷题”走向“有计划地训练”。
The platform stores local AMC and BMO1 attempt data and generates topic-level diagnostics, helping students move from simply doing problems to training with a plan.

题目来源说明 / Source Notice

题目与答案来源
Problem and Answer Sources

本平台所整理的 AMC 10/12 题目、答案与解析链接来源于 Art of Problem Solving（AoPS）公开页面。 平台仅用于学习与训练目的，并在题目页面保留原始来源链接，便于学生回到 AoPS 查看原始题目、答案页和解析页。
AMC 10/12 problems, answer keys, and solution links are organized from publicly available Art of Problem Solving（AoPS）pages. This platform is for learning and practice. Source links are retained so students can review the original AoPS problem, answer key, and solution pages.

BMO1 训练题来自用户提供的《BMO1分类题库2000-2023》PDF，并参考 BMOS/UKMT 官方公开页面整理来源链接。未找到开放官方答案的题目会明确标注，不进行自动判分。
BMO1 practice problems are imported from the provided BMO1 classified PDF, with source links organized from public BMOS/UKMT pages where available. Problems without open official answers are marked clearly and are not auto-graded.
