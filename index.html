AMC/BMO 平台说明 / About the Platform

AMC 10/12 & BMO1 Practice Center

总平台 / Platform
About This Platform

面向 AMC 10/12 与 BMO1 备赛的校内数学训练平台
A School-Based AMC 10/12 and BMO1 Training Platform

本平台由数学社维护，面向准备 AMC 10/12 与 BMO1 的同学提供系统化真题练习、AMC 全卷模拟和学习表现分析。 AMC 模块用于选择题训练与限时模拟；BMO1 模块作为独立入口，用于证明题分类训练并直接展示答案与解析。
This platform is maintained by the Math Club. It supports students preparing for AMC 10/12 and BMO1 through structured past-paper practice, AMC full-length mock exams, and performance diagnostics. BMO1 is kept as a separate proof-problem practice mode with answers and solutions shown directly.

维护方 / Maintained By

由数学社维护
Maintained by the Math Club

平台内容整理、功能迭代和使用反馈收集由数学社持续维护，用于支持校内数学学习共同体建设。
The Math Club maintains content organization, feature updates, and feedback collection to support the school mathematics community.

题库范围 / Problem Bank

收录 AMC 10/12 与 BMO1 训练题
AMC 10/12 and BMO1 Problems

AMC 题库按年份、卷别、题号、知识点和难度组织；BMO1 题库覆盖 2000–2023 年官方真题，并作为独立训练入口。
AMC problems are organized by paper and topic; BMO1 includes official problems from 2000–2023 in a separate practice mode.

练习方式 / Practice Modes

支持 AMC 练习与 BMO 证明题训练
AMC Practice and BMO Proof Training

AMC 提供单题练习与 75 分钟全卷模拟；BMO1 按年份、知识点和难度筛选，直接查看答案与分阶段解析。
AMC supports single practice and timed mocks; BMO1 supports filtered proof-problem practice with staged solutions.

学习分析 / Learning Analytics

适合 AMC 前置训练
Built for AMC Readiness

平台会在本机保存作答记录，并根据正确率生成知识点表现与复习建议，帮助学生从“刷题”走向“有计划地训练”。
The platform stores local attempt data and generates topic-level diagnostics, helping students move from simply doing problems to training with a plan.

题目来源说明 / Source Notice

题目与答案来源
Problem and Answer Sources

本平台所整理的 AMC 10/12 题目、答案与解析链接来源于 Art of Problem Solving（AoPS）公开页面。 平台仅用于学习与训练目的，并在题目页面保留原始来源链接，便于学生回到 AoPS 查看原始题目、答案页和解析页。
AMC 10/12 problems, answer keys, and solution links are organized from publicly available Art of Problem Solving（AoPS）pages. This platform is for learning and practice. Source links are retained so students can review the original AoPS problem, answer key, and solution pages.

BMO1 题干来自 BMOS/UKMT 官方历年题目汇编，知识点分类参考《BMO1分类题库2000-2023》PDF。未找到开放官方答案的题目会明确标注，不进行自动判分。
BMO1 statements come from the official BMOS/UKMT archive, with topic classification informed by the classified BMO1 PDF. Items without an open answer are clearly marked and are not auto-graded.
