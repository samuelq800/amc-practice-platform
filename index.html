平台说明 / About the Platform

AMC 10/12 Practice Center

About This Platform

面向 AMC 10/12 备赛的校内数学训练平台
A School-Based AMC 10/12 Training Platform

本平台由数学社维护，面向准备 AMC 10/12 的同学提供系统化真题练习、全卷模拟和学习表现分析。 平台重点服务 AMC 前置训练，帮助学生熟悉竞赛题型、建立限时作答节奏，并通过知识点诊断明确下一步复习方向。
This platform is maintained by the Math Club. It supports students preparing for AMC 10/12 through structured past-paper practice, full-length mock exams, and performance diagnostics. It is designed for AMC readiness training: students can learn the contest format, build timed-exam habits, and identify what to review next.

维护方 / Maintained By

由数学社维护
Maintained by the Math Club

平台内容整理、功能迭代和使用反馈收集由数学社持续维护，用于支持校内数学学习共同体建设。
The Math Club maintains content organization, feature updates, and feedback collection to support the school mathematics community.

题库范围 / Problem Bank

收录 AMC 10/12 历年真题
AMC 10/12 Past Problems

题库覆盖 AMC 10 与 AMC 12 的历年试卷，并按年份、卷别、题号、知识点和难度区间组织，便于针对性训练。
The problem bank includes AMC 10 and AMC 12 papers, organized by year, form, problem number, topic, and difficulty band.

练习方式 / Practice Modes

支持单题练习与全卷模拟
Single Practice and Full Mock Exams

单题练习适合按知识点查漏补缺；全卷模拟提供 75 分钟倒计时、统一交卷和 AMC 规则模拟计分，更接近正式比赛体验。
Single-problem practice supports targeted review, while full mock exams use a 75-minute timer, exam submission, and AMC-style scoring.

学习分析 / Learning Analytics

适合 AMC 前置训练
Built for AMC Readiness

平台会在本机保存作答记录，并根据正确率生成知识点表现与复习建议，帮助学生从“刷题”走向“有计划地训练”。
The platform stores local attempt data and generates topic-level diagnostics, helping students move from simply doing problems to training with a plan.

题目来源说明 / Source Notice

题目与答案来源
Problem and Answer Sources

本平台所整理的 AMC 10/12 题目、答案与解析链接来源于 Art of Problem Solving（AoPS）公开页面。 平台仅用于学习与训练目的，并在题目页面保留原始来源链接，便于学生回到 AoPS 查看原始题目、答案页和解析页。
AMC 10/12 problems, answer keys, and solution links are organized from publicly available Art of Problem Solving（AoPS）pages. This platform is for learning and practice. Source links are retained so students can review the original AoPS problem, answer key, and solution pages.
